What is verified, and what isn't

A buyer should know exactly how much each "approved" is worth. This page says what this build really checks, what it simulates, and what it assumes.

How well a fact is proven

Every key fact (GSTIN, legal name, PAN, bank account) gets a level. A valid-looking number is only the start: someone can write a valid PAN on a blank sheet of paper.

Simulated answers are labelled in every run and can never raise a fact to A4. Free sandboxes for these APIs need a registered business's KYC, which a candidate can't provide.

How the decision is made

  • Rules decide; the AI only reads documents. Every outcome traces to a named rule with its evidence.
  • Order: out of scope → reject → internal review → vendor fixes → approved. Approval requires every required rule to pass.
  • Reject only for an identity conflict (e.g. a GSTIN belonging to a different PAN) or the blocklist, and a person confirms before the vendor is told.
  • Authenticity heuristics never reject. A suspected fake goes to a person; the vendor gets a neutral "under review" message so a fraudster learns nothing.
  • "Couldn't read clearly" is never treated as "read and different": unclear documents mean "please upload a clearer copy". Misread characters are corrected only where provable (a PAN's letter positions, the GSTIN check digit); account numbers are never corrected.
  • The employee who requested a vendor (the requester) can't approve it. High-risk vendors (≥ ₹50 lakh a year, or any fraud signal) need the Finance controller.
  • Approval is not payment: invoices are still paid only after PO and goods-receipt matching (out of scope here).

Tested on imperfect real-world inputs

Blurry, compressed, tilted copies of genuine documents

30 degraded copies of 6 genuine documents (blur, low-quality JPEG, 40% resolution, 3° tilt, phone-like): 60 of 60 fields read exactly, 0 wrong, and no fraud flag on any real genuine document.

Real fakes, made or found for testing
  • PAN hand-written on paper → vendor asked for the original
  • Photo of a screen, screenshot of a certificate → vendor asked for the file
  • Certificate edited in Canva → signature gone, editor recorded: fraud class
  • AI-generated PAN card and cheque → fraud class

Assumptions and limits

  • Vendors are Indian and GST-registered; others go to a manual path.
  • All demo vendors are fictional. Their documents carry a SPECIMEN banner and are recognised by fingerprint, because a fictional PAN card can't legally carry the national emblem.
  • No real person's documents from the internet are used. The genuine signed certificate is a government office's own published document.
  • Gemini's free tier allows 15 reads a minute; at most 2 applications run at once. If the AI is down, a saved reading of the same file is used and labelled.
  • No logins in the demo: the "Acting as" menu stands in for single sign-on so you can see who may do what.
